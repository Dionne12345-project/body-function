01 / OUR APPROACH

Care that sees the whole you.

At Body Function, we believe good physiotherapy goes beyond treating symptoms. We take time to understand your goals, how you move and what matters most to your everyday life.

Led by Dionne Jules, our approach brings together individual assessment, evidence-informed treatment and progressive rehabilitation, delivered in the comfort of your home across our service area.

02 / MEET YOUR PHYSIOTHERAPIST

Meet Dionne.

Personalised physiotherapy, built around your movement, strength and recovery.

Dionne graduated with a BSc (Hons) in Physiotherapy and has since gained several years of experience in both NHS and private musculoskeletal physiotherapy.

She has worked with adults and children across a range of musculoskeletal, orthopaedic and post-operative conditions, as both an adult and children's specialist physiotherapist.

Dionne has a particular interest in lower-limb rehabilitation, spinal pain and headaches. She enjoys helping patients improve their strength, mobility and function throughout their rehabilitation journey.

Alongside her physiotherapy experience, Dionne has a background in personal training and Clinical Pilates, with a strong interest in exercise, movement and building strength.

Education & qualifications

  • BSc (Hons), Physiotherapy
  • FSEM Diploma in Musculoskeletal Medicine (DipMSK)
  • Level 3 Clinical Pilates Instructor
  • Level 3 Personal Training
  • Level 3 Exercise for Health Specialist
  • Level 4 Lower Back Pain Specialist
